Conclusion
 

  • Outliers make a few large, concentrated bets on long shots
     
  • Fewer outliers are profitable than other wallets (45.6% vs 57.5%).
     
  • Outliers are a mix of potential insiders and high-risk gamblers

  • Focus on outliers that win their long-shot bets 

Main Takeaways

  • A wallet’s opening bet, mainly its entry price, predicts its profitability.

     
  • Wallet history provides little information about a wallet's profitability

     
  • Trading behaviour can identify outliers

     
  • Outliers are a combination of potential inside traders and high-risk gamblers

     
  • Next step: focus on profitable outliers
