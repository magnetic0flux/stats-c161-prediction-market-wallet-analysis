Where the outliers come from

Wallets grouped by average bet size and by long-shot share (long shot = price below 0.20). Each cell shows how many times more likely its wallets are to be outliers than the overall rate.

What predicts being an outlier

Logistic regression of outlier status on standardised features. Odds ratio per one standard deviation, holding the other features fixed; the line is the 95% interval, the dashed line is no effect.

Outliers vs everyone else
